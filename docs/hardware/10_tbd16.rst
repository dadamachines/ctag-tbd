******
TBD-16
******

.. raw:: html

   <img src="../_static/assets/dadamachines-tbd-16_tilt.jpg" alt="The dadamachines TBD-16 seen from the front: four encoders under the OLED display, 32 buttons with LEDs, and SD card, headphone, USB-C and USB host ports on the front edge." style="max-width: 100%; width: 100%;">

The TBD-16 is a compact 16-track groovebox from Berlin-based dadamachines: a
standalone instrument for synthesis, sampling, sequencing and effects in a
110 × 110 × 25 mm case. It is built on the open-source CTAG TBD audio platform.
Plug in USB-C power, connect speakers or headphones, and start making music.
No computer, no Eurorack case, no setup required.

At a glance
===========

.. list-table::
   :widths: 30 70
   :header-rows: 0

   * - **Sound**
     - 16 tracks, 2 FX tracks and a master track; more than 40 sound machines
       and effects
   * - **Controls**
     - 4 endless potentiometers, buttons with RGB feedback, a volume control
       and a 2.4″ OLED display
   * - **Processing**
     - ESP32-P4 for audio, RP2350B for UI and MIDI, ESP32-C6 for Wi-Fi.
       44.1 kHz, 32-bit float internal processing
   * - **Audio**
     - Two stereo inputs, stereo main output, separate headphone/cue output
   * - **MIDI and sync**
     - 2× TRS MIDI in, 2× TRS MIDI out (Type-A), USB MIDI, USB host,
       Ableton Link, Eurorack clock/reset
   * - **Storage**
     - Built-in, user-replaceable microSD card for projects and samples
   * - **Power**
     - USB-C (5 V); works with any USB power bank, mount included
   * - **Case**
     - 110 × 110 × 25 mm, anodised aluminium top, powder-coated steel base

Wireless connectivity is Wi-Fi and Ableton Link.


Connections
===========

All MIDI connections follow the `TRS Type-A standard <https://minimidi.world/>`_,
so no adapters or special cables are needed.

.. tip::

   For wireless tempo sync without MIDI cables, use Ableton Link instead of
   MIDI clock.
